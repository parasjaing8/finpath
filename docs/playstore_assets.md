## Research Findings

### Competitive Landscape

**Who dominates India finance on Play Store (2026):**
- PhonePe, Google Pay — UPI giants, not competitors
- Groww — "India's No.1 Stock Broker" positioning, trading-focused, free demat CTA
- INDmoney — "Track all your wealth in one place", net worth angle, 10M+ users
- ET Money — SIP/mutual fund focus, SEBI RIA badge, "smart investing" framing
- MoneyView — SMS-read-based expense tracking
- None of them lead with FIRE. None are local-only. None have a charity angle.

**Competitive gap FinPath fills:**
- Only app that calculates and surfaces your actual FIRE date
- Only major finance app that is 100% local (no cloud, no data harvesting)
- Only finance app with a charitable mission (51% profits)
- Built for Indian tax/instrument context (PPF, NPS, NSE/BSE) — not a Western app reskinned

**What competitor listings look like:**
- Groww: green brand, clean white screenshots, bold number callouts ("0 brokerage"), social proof header
- INDmoney: dark navy, net worth number front and center in screenshot 1, wealth aggregation story
- ET Money: trust signals heavy (SEBI, AMFI badges), muted tones, feature-grid screenshots
- Pattern: all lead with a BIG NUMBER (portfolio value, returns, savings) in screenshot 1

**Play Store ASO — 2026 best practices:**
- First 2-3 screenshots carry ~90% of conversion weight — users don't scroll
- Screenshot 1 must communicate core value in under 2 seconds
- Short description appears in search results — front-load the differentiator
- Minimum 2 screenshots required; 6-8 is optimal
- Recommended size: 1080 × 1920px (9:16 portrait)
- Feature graphic: 1024 × 500px, JPEG or 24-bit PNG, max 8MB
- Avoid words "free", "best", "new", "#1" in copy (Play Store policy risk)
- No pricing references in screenshots
- Consistent color palette and typography across all screenshots

### Long Description (max 4000 chars)

---

**What if you knew the exact date you could stop working?**

FinPath calculates your FIRE date — the day your investments generate enough to cover your expenses forever. Enter your numbers once. See your financial independence date immediately. Then watch it move as your net worth grows.

**Built for India. Built for privacy. Built for the FIRE generation.**

---

**FIRE Calculator — Your Number, Your Date**

Most retirement calculators tell you to "save more." FinPath tells you *when* you're free. Input your corpus, monthly savings, and expenses. The engine — adjusted for Indian inflation (6-7%) and real equity returns — calculates your FIRE date to the month. As your net worth grows, your FIRE date moves forward. That's the motivation no spreadsheet gives you.

---

**Core Features**

- **FIRE Date Calculator** — See your financial independence date in real time. Adjusted for Indian inflation and market returns, not Western benchmarks.

- **Net Worth Dashboard** — Your total picture: assets minus liabilities, tracked over time. See the number that actually matters.

- **Asset Portfolio Tracker** — Stocks (NSE/BSE), mutual funds, fixed deposits, PPF, NPS, EPF, gold, real estate — every asset category that matters in India.

- **SIP Projections** — Model what different monthly SIP amounts do to your FIRE date. See 10-year, 20-year, and retirement corpus projections.

- **Expense Tracker** — Log monthly spend by category. FinPath uses this to refine your FIRE number automatically.

- **Multiple Profiles** — Track household finances separately. Compare scenarios. Plan together.

- **CSV Export** — Full data export, always. Your data is yours.

---

**100% Private. Zero Cloud. Always.**

FinPath stores everything on your device. There is no server. There is no account to create. There is no company that can read your financial data — because we never receive it.

No login screen. No "sync to cloud." No email required.

Your net worth figures, FIRE projections, and expense history exist only on your phone. If you uninstall the app, the data is gone — because it was never anywhere else.

In an era where every app wants your financial data to sell you products, FinPath has chosen to be structurally incapable of doing that.

---

**51% of Profits Go to Charity**

This is written into how FinPath operates — not as a marketing line, but as a commitment. More than half of every rupee of profit from this app is donated to verified charitable causes.

A finance app that helps you build wealth while redistributing it. That's the mission.

### Screenshot 1 — HOOK: FIRE Date Reveal

**Screen to capture:** FIRE calculator results screen showing the FIRE date prominently (e.g., "March 2041") with corpus target and years remaining.

**Overlay headline:** `Your financial freedom date.`
*(Large, white, bold — centered top third)*

**Sub-caption:** `FinPath calculates exactly when you can stop working.`
*(Medium weight, below headline)*

**Background/frame:** Deep forest green (#1B5E20) device frame. White text overlay. The app screen itself shows the FIRE date number in large type.

**Design notes:** The FIRE date (e.g., "March 2041" or "14 years away") must be the largest element on screen. This is the hook — mimic INDmoney's pattern of leading with a big, personal number. The viewer must immediately think "I want to know MY date."

**Emotional target:** The banner should feel like a fintech brand that's serious about your money — not a get-rich-quick app, not a bank. Think: calm confidence, not excitement.
